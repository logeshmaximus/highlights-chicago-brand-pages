Same Generac, different hookup. What changes on the visit.

Set by the panel and the load calculation
  1. ATSAutomatic transfer switch

    Beside the main panel, carrying chosen circuits or the whole panel. Generac sells them from 100 to 800 amps.

  2. Service-ratedSwitch at the meter

    Becomes the main disconnect ahead of the panel, so the whole house transfers at once. ComEd disconnects the service to fit it.

  3. Load modulesLoad management

    Big loads such as central air take turns, so a smaller Generac can carry a whole house.

Generac services · 15 in 4 groups

Every Generac service Highlights Chicago does

From a new standby hookup to a unit that stayed off in a storm. The electrical side, permitted and inspected.

01

Installation

From the load calculation to a unit that starts on its own.

  • New Generac standby installation

    Load calculation, pad, transfer switch, permitted feeder and a tested startup.

  • Generac transfer switch installation

    A switch matched to the panel, on its own or with a new unit.

  • Generator wiring and feeder

    Conduit from the pad to the switch, through brick or under a walk.

  • Gas line coordination

    The gas line is a separate trade on its own permit; the electrical work is scheduled around it.

02

Repair & alarms

Faults traced on the electrical side, from the controller to the switch.

  • Won’t start in an outage

    A weak battery, the controller left out of AUTO, a closed gas valve, or an Overcrank alarm.

  • Runs, but the house stays dark

    The transfer switch did not transfer, or the generator’s own breaker is off.

  • Battery and charger circuit

    The starting battery and the circuit that keeps it charged.

  • Mobile Link shows offline

    Lost Wi-Fi at the pad or a changed router password; the unit itself may be fine.

03

Maintenance

The electrical side of the schedule in Generac’s owner’s manual.

  • Generac maintenance visit

    Battery charger circuit, transfer switch and wiring, on Generac’s schedule.

  • Weekly exercise reset

    The exercise day and time set again on the controller, for example after a battery change.

  • Winter readiness

    Generac’s cold-weather kit adds battery and oil heaters for single-digit starts.

  • A Generac that came with the house

    Permit record, switch, breaker and feeder checked before anyone relies on it.

04

Batteries & upgrades

When the panel or the backup plan needs more than a generator.

  • Generac PWRcell battery

    The battery tie-in at the main panel, with its own backup circuits.

  • Service or panel upgrade

    When the panel is full or obsolete, the upgrade and the hookup are one job. See panel upgrades.

  • Portable generator inlet

    An inlet box and a transfer switch or listed interlock, so only one source feeds the house.

01Does a Chicago house need the biggest Generac?

No. The circuits that protect a Chicago house in an outage are few: the furnace or boiler, the sump pump, the refrigerator and some lights. A smaller Generac on those circuits covers the damage risk, and everything above that is comfort. Highlights Chicago can price both sizes from one walkthrough, before the household decides.

02Is a Generac dealer needed, or a licensed electrician?

Generac sells home standby units through authorized dealers, listed on Generac’s own dealer locator. The licensed part of the job is the electrical work Highlights Chicago does: the load calculation, the transfer switch, the feeder, the City permit and the inspection. How the unit itself is supplied is worth settling in the estimate, before anything is ordered.

03Air-cooled Guardian or a liquid-cooled Generac?

Most houses fit an air-cooled Guardian model, which Generac sells from about 10 to 26 kilowatts. Liquid-cooled models serve larger loads and longer run times, at a higher price and with more maintenance. Highlights Chicago lets the load calculation decide which side of that line a house falls on.

04Does a Generac need a City permit in Chicago?

Yes. The City’s electrical permit form records each permanent generator by its kilowatt rating, and the gas piping is permitted separately. An unpermitted Generac tends to surface during a home sale, and the inspection is the only outside check that the transfer switch was wired correctly.

05Can a Generac run on propane in Chicago?

Yes, most home Generac models run on natural gas or propane. Chicago houses usually use natural gas from the street main, since a propane tank needs yard space, clearances and refills that a city lot rarely offers. Generac rates many models slightly lower on natural gas than on propane, so the fuel is settled before the size.

06How often does a Generac need maintenance?

Generac’s owner’s manual sets service intervals by run hours and by years, covering oil, filters, spark plugs and the battery. Newer controllers flag a maintenance reminder on the display. Highlights Chicago handles the electrical side of a service visit: the battery charger circuit, the transfer switch and the wiring.
